Who are we?
Felix & Coco
Tested, tasted & approved.

We're Felix & Coco, two 25-year-old Amsterdammers with a love for good food, good wine and lively hotspots.

On (W)etenwaar you’ll find everything we like. There isn’t really a clear line to it. One morning we’re having a toastie in the conservatory at Hoppe, and the weekend after we’re standing there with a beer in hand. We love cosy eetcafés with a nice interior, but every now and then it can be a bit fancier too.

From a simple drink to a long dinner: price doesn’t matter much to us, as long as the place is good. So you’ll find a bit of everything here, but all the places have one thing in common: we’ve been there ourselves.

No sponsored lists, just our personal favourites.
